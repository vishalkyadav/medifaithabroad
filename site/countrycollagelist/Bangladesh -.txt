    <div class="uzb-admission-process">
  <h2>🇧🇩 Admission Process for MBBS in Bangladesh</h2>
  <p class="sub-heading">✨ Embark on Your Medical Dream!</p>

  <div class="process-step">
    <h3>1. Spark Your Journey: Check Your Eligibility</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>PCB aspirant?</strong> Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology—and at least <strong>60% in PCB</strong> (55% for reserved categories)</li>
      <li><strong>NEET?</strong> Qualifying NEET score is mandatory for Indian students</li>
      <li>Must be <strong>17 years or older</strong> by December 31 of the admission year</li>
      <li><strong>No Drop year</strong> allowed</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="process-step">
    <h3>2. Select Your Stage: Pick the Ideal College</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Choose from NMC-, BMDC-, WHO-approved institutions</li>
      <li>Options include government colleges like <strong>Dhaka Medical, Chittagong, Rajshahi</strong></li>
      <li>Or private colleges like <strong>East West, Tairunnessa, Jahurul Islam</strong></li>
      <li>Compare fees, infrastructure, clinical exposure, and hostel facilities</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="process-step">
    <h3>3. Apply with Purpose: Submit Your Documents</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Required: 10th & 12th mark sheets, NEET scorecard, passport copy, birth certificate, equivalency certificate, photos</li>
      <li>Some colleges ask for extra docs (insurance, medical fitness)—check early!</li>
      <li><strong>Pro Tip:</strong> Ensure documents are attested/authorized if needed</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="process-step">
    <h3>4. Get the Golden Ticket: Admission Letter</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>After review, expect Admission Letter in <strong>6–8 weeks</strong></li>
      <li>This confirms enrollment and enables visa & seat confirmation</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="process-step">
    <h3>5. Secure Your Seat: Fee Payment</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Pay first-year tuition (or full course, depending on college) to lock your seat</li>
      <li>Installment/lump-sum options vary by college</li>
      <li>Budget: ₹25–40 lakhs total (tuition, hostel, food, books)</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="process-step">
    <h3>6. Visa Voyage: Apply and Travel</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Apply for student visa with Admission Letter + passport at Bangladesh High Commission</li>
      <li>Processing time: ~60 days</li>
      <li>Once approved—book flights, pack, and get ready!</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="process-step">
    <h3>7. Arrive & Thrive: Campus Enrollment</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Submit original docs at college</li>
      <li>Receive Student ID, attend orientation, move into hostel</li>
      <li>Most colleges provide Indian-style mess and secure accommodations</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="process-step">
    <h3>8. Journey to Doctorhood: Study, Train & Intern</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>MBBS = 5 years academics + 1-year internship (NMC approved)</li>
      <li>Medium of instruction: <strong>English</strong></li>
      <li>Early clinical exposure ensures strong hands-on training</li>
      <li>Degree valid for FMGE/NExT registration in India</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
