Introduction

China hosts many public medical universities popular with international students. The Medifaith Abroad reference site describes English or bilingual tracks and university-specific fees in yuan. Requirements and costs vary widely—confirm each program’s language of instruction, duration, and NMC alignment before you apply.

Why China

Why students consider China

Factors commonly discussed with families during MBBS abroad counselling.

  • Large university ecosystem

    Reference materials list numerous state medical universities across major cities.

  • English-medium options

    Many programs are advertised in English; some combine English and Chinese study.

  • Established international intake

    China has long welcomed international medical students; counselling helps navigate choices.

  • Clear document checklist

    Reference admission steps include PCB results, NEET, and standard identity documents.

Universities

University information

The reference China page includes a large university table with annual tuition and hostel ranges in yuan. We help you shortlist institutions matching your budget and profile without endorsing any single college. Always verify medium of instruction and clinical training language on the official prospectus.

Cost planning

Reference materials cite approximate annual tuition from roughly ¥21,000 upward depending on university, plus hostel and living costs. Exchange rates and annual revisions apply. Do not rely on website tables alone—obtain the university’s current fee letter for your intake.

Figures are indicative only—confirm current fees and charges with the university before payment.

Requirements

Eligibility and application requirements

  • 12th with PCB, minimum 50% per reference site for Indian applicants.
  • NEET qualification required per reference admission table.
  • Some universities may require English proficiency tests.

Requirements can change by intake and institution—verify with official university and regulatory sources.

Documents typically required

  • 10th and 12th mark sheets
  • NEET scorecard
  • Passport
  • Photographs
  • English test results (if required)

Student life

Living and accommodation

Accommodation arrangements depend on the university and city in China. Hostel or shared housing is commonly discussed during counselling; availability, cost, and rules vary by institution. Confirm what is included in your admission offer and budget for living expenses separately.

FAQ

China — frequently asked questions

Is instruction fully in English?
Programs differ; some are English-only and others include Chinese. Confirm on the official program page.
How long is the course?
Duration depends on the university and regulatory framework. Confirm the academic calendar and internship length before enrolling.
What about licensing in India?
Graduates must meet NMC requirements and pass the applicable screening/licensing exam. Plan this separately from admission.
Can Medifaith secure a seat?
We support applications; universities issue offers based on their own review.
